# interests summary

what the user is into, what they are learning, and what they keep coming back to. this exists
so an agent can be useful without asking the same orienting questions every session.

## dormant

_none recorded._ interests do not get deleted when they go quiet — they get dated and moved
here, because they tend to come back.

## rules for this domain

- an interest is `epistemic: preference`, dated, and revisable.
- do not infer an interest from a single mention, and do not infer that a stated interest
  means the user wants suggestions about it.
- tools and links go to [resources](../85-resources/resources.md), not here.
