## rules for this domain

- **unlogged days are excluded from averages**, never counted as zero. a week with two logged
  days reports "2 of 7 days logged" alongside any average, or reports nothing.
- quantities the agent estimated are marked `estimated: true` with the assumption preserved
  ("assumed a standard 30 g serving"). the user's own numbers are never overwritten by an
  estimate.
- if a missing detail materially changes the value, ask one focused question instead of
  guessing.
- weight trends exclude missing weigh-ins rather than interpolating them.
- no target, deficit, or macro split is invented. those come from the user or from a source.
