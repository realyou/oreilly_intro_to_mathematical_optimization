# goal name

## outcome
what is true when this is achieved.

## definition of done
the unambiguous test. someone else could check it.

## baseline
where things stand now, dated. without this, progress is unmeasurable.

## target
the specific target, and the measurement method.

## rationale
why this matters. this is the section that decides whether to keep going when it gets hard.

## success criteria
a small number, each measurable.

## milestones
checkpoints, with the next one marked.

## supporting projects and workstreams

## supporting habits
habits support the goal. they do not prove it — never claim habit completion caused the
outcome.

## constraints

## review cadence
when, and what gets reviewed.

## adjustment trigger
the condition that means the plan should change rather than be pushed harder.

## progress
from criteria or milestones. never from task count or elapsed time.

## status history
every change, with a date and a reason. pauses and abandonments especially.
